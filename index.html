The 3R Video Game Challenge is an educational competition that combines video game technology with environmental education. Designed to foster sustainability awareness among students, The challenge promotes the principles of Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle through interactive missions and virtual challenges. Sign up!!

TOURNAMENT DETAILS

MAY 27 / 2026 TOURNAMENT START

DAY 1

MAY 27

Once confirmed, players will receive an email with the link to the 3R Discord server where they will find information about their matches and mission schedules.

11:00 - 14:00

Orientation session and game familiarization. Presentation of sustainability objectives and tournament rules.

15:00 - 18:00

First competition round. Participants will complete missions focused on waste reduction and responsible consumption.

19:00 - 20:00

Feedback session and announcement of qualifiers for the next phase.

DAY 2

MAY 28

Finalists will play in Virtual Reality to complete the final missions.

11:00 - 14:00

Semi-finals with missions focused on creative reuse and innovative solutions for environmental problems.

15:00 - 18:00

Virtual Reality finals with high-complexity challenges about recycling and circular economy.

19:00 - 22:00

Award ceremony and event closing with presentation of outstanding projects.
